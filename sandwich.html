DAAD Sandwich Program

Duration: 2026/03 – 2027/02

Host Institutions: HITS PSO Group (Heidelberg, Germany) & NTU GIA (Taipei, Taiwan)

Advisor: Prof. Friedrich “Fritz” Röpke

Research Focus: Multidimensional radiation-hydrodynamics simulations of shock breakout in binary-formed circumstellar media (CSM) with CASTRO + MGFLD, and observational diagnostics via Sedona Monte Carlo spectra.

Program Overview

The DAAD Sandwich Program extends the summer project into a year-long collaborative study between NTU and HITS. This phase refines the confined-shell CSM models, integrates stellar evolution (via MESA-generated opacity tables), and establishes predictive light-curve libraries for red-supergiant (RSG) and blue-supergiant (BSG) explosions. The work bridges numerical radiation-hydrodynamics and early-time observational data analysis, strengthening cross-institutional research between Taiwan and Germany.
