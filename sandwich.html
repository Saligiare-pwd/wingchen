DAAD Sandwich Program

A year-long research collaboration connecting HITS in Heidelberg with NTU in Taipei.

Duration: March 2026 – February 2027

Host institutions: HITS PSO Group, Heidelberg & NTU GIA, Taipei

Advisor: Prof. Friedrich “Fritz” Röpke

Research focus: Multidimensional radiation-hydrodynamics simulations of shock breakout in binary-formed circumstellar media, with observational diagnostics using CASTRO, MGFLD, and Sedona.
